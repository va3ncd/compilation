EVIDENCE REVIEW & PHYSICAL ANALYSIS

Motor Vehicle Collision

9th Line & 5 Sideroad
Halton Hills, Ontario

A structured review of the available documentary, photographic, digital, witness, vehicle, roadway and physical evidence concerning the collision involving a 2019 GMC Acadia SLT and a 2010 Toyota Venza.

Scope and Methodology

The direct video recording of the collision is no longer available. In addition, certain potentially relevant data from the vehicle's onboard electronic systems were not preserved as part of the original investigation. As a result, a direct reconstruction based on a continuous video record or complete vehicle-event data is not possible.

This report therefore undertakes an indirect reconstruction of the events leading to and following the collision, using the independent evidence that remains available.

Particular care has been taken to identify, preserve and document information that may have a bearing on the reconstruction, including:

  • contemporaneous police documentation and subsequent revisions;
  • independently recorded vehicle-location and movement data;
  • recorded vehicle speeds and changes in motion;
  • the timing and sequence of traffic signals at the intersection;
  • physical positions and final trajectories of the vehicles;
  • vehicle damage patterns and recovered vehicle components;
  • photographs and video recordings of the collision scene;
  • witness statements and the documented locations and circumstances of the witnesses;
  • vehicle specifications relevant to the physical analysis; and
  • publicly documented technical information concerning the vehicles and recording systems.

presented as such. Where a conclusion depends upon physical modelling, inference or assumptions, those assumptions and the resulting limitations are identified explicitly.

The purpose of this approach is not to replace missing direct evidence with speculation, but to determine what can and cannot be established from the independent evidence that remains available, and to test the proposed sequences of events against that evidence.

Because additional information or documentation may become available, this report is maintained as a structured working document. Evidence identifiers and source references are intended to remain stable as the organization of the report develops.\
